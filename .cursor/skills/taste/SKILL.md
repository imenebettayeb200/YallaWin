# Taste — YallaWin

The site should feel like a night-out poster: dark, warm, and quiet until the accent color shows up.

## Color

- Background is deep navy (`#06071a` on the dashboard, a photo wash on the home page). Never a flat gray page.
- Text is white. Secondary text is white at about 55% opacity. Do not introduce gray-on-gray.
- One accent: the pink (`#ff2e7e`) to orange (`#ff9a1f`) gradient. Use it on the main action only (sign-in is white; "Dashboard" and "Create" can be the gradient).
- Success is green (`#35d07f`). Warnings on countdowns are warm gold (`#ffd27a`). Errors are `#ff4d4f`.

## Type

- UI is Inter. Weights 500–700. Buttons are sentence case.
- Instrument Serif, often italic, is only for the welcome name and the logo. Do not set tables or form labels in serif.
- Big headlines stay around 42px on the home hero. Dashboard titles stay smaller so the numbers can be the focus.

## Layout

- Home content is centered, max about 1180px, with 32px side padding.
- Cards are portrait, cover-first, with the title sitting on the image. Keep the heart in the top corner and the price as a small pill.
- Dashboard panels are translucent navy with a 1px white border at low opacity and a 16px radius.
- Space sections with 24–36px. Do not stack equal gaps everywhere; the hero can breathe more than a table row.

## Motion

- One live detail is enough: a small green pulse next to the clock, or a carousel that moves every few seconds.
- Hover states lighten a surface. Do not bounce, spin, or scale cards past a slight lift.
- Toasts sit bottom-right and leave on their own.

## Copy

- Short and specific. "6 events on sale" is better than "Discover amazing experiences".
- Prices are in TND. Dates read like "Sat, 22 Nov".
- Empty states say what to do next ("Try another category") instead of a joke.

## Do not

- Do not add purple gradients, glassmorphism blur stacks, or a second font.
- Do not put more than one primary button in the same bar.
- Do not hide the price, the city, or how many spots are left.
